### 🧭 Rumbo

**Your whole event in one QR.**

Free sign-up or a ticket, a pass whose QR changes every 20 seconds so a screenshot won't get anyone in, a door and a lunch counter that keep working without signal, sponsor booths with a route and a prize, and the reports a sponsor or a Stellar ambassador chapter asks for. Every check-in and every redemption leaves its footprint on Stellar for a fraction of a cent, and nobody has to hold crypto: Rumbo sponsors every fee. Open source, running on the Stellar testnet.

### 🎓 Nemi

**Your chapter, as a lesson video your students finish.**

A teacher uploads a textbook chapter or types a topic, and Nemi turns it into a short animated lesson: illustrated scenes in one consistent style, diagrams whose parts light up exactly when the narrator names them, step-by-step math, Manim animations, quiz pauses and captions, with a local voice. The teacher edits every scene. Muse Spark watches the finished video, leaves notes pinned to the minute and can fix them itself. Students get a link where the video stops at each quiz, and the teacher sees their answers with Muse Spark's advice on what to reteach.

Built on Meta's Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, SAM 3.1 and Muse Voice Transcribe, with Manim and Remotion. Lessons in Spanish, English and Portuguese, and a studio a teacher understands on first use.

<a href="https://github.com/nema1502/nemi"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nema1502/nemi/main/docs/media/diagram.gif" alt="A volcano diagram whose parts light up as the narrator names them" width="640"></a>

**Nemi for events.** The same engines for people who run hackathons and communities: bring your logos and Nemi drafts the launch kit, a 9-post Instagram grid that forms one picture, stories and WhatsApp statuses, a Luma cover, a profile picture and the copy for every network.

### 🦙 Tinkazo

**Draws anyone can check.**

The list is sealed on Stellar before the random number exists. A Soroban contract verifies a future drand round on chain, and anyone can redo the result from their phone. Free to enter, always. Twelve games on the big screen, eight from Bolivia and Latin America.

### 🛠️ What I build

- **Educational video:** an animation engine on Manim and Remotion, with automatic auditors that catch what the eye misses, and the pedagogy behind my own explainer series.
- **AI products and automation:** multimodal agents on Meta's Muse models, n8n agents and workflows that save teams real hours.
- **Smart contracts on Stellar:** Soroban in Rust, on-chain verification of drand's BLS signatures (CAP-59), deterministic and testable protocols.
- **Event infrastructure on Stellar:** passes with a signed QR, check-ins and redemptions recorded on chain, an offline scanner that syncs when the signal comes back, and sponsored fees.
- **dApps:** TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS, Stellar SDK, Freighter and Google sign-in with Pollar.
